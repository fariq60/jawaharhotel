A TASTE OF TIMELESS OLD DELHI

Rooted in tradition, loved across generations.

Born near the historic Jama Masjid, Jawahar Hotel has upheld the highest culinary standards of Old Delhi's traditional cooking.

01 / SLOW COOKING

Traditional Degh Preparation

Gravies are simmered slowly in heavy deghs with whole spices, rich marrow, and pure ghee to achieve the authentic depth of Purani Dilli recipes.

02 / CHARCOAL GRILL

Clay Tandoor Roasts

Tender Kalmi Kababs, Mutton Burra, and Seekh Kababs are freshly prepared over charcoal embers for that distinct smoky flavour.

03 / FRESH BREADS

Fresh Khamiri & Sheermal

Fluffy, golden tandoor-baked Khamiri Rotis and saffron Sheermal baked fresh on order to pair with rich curries.
