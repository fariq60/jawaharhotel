Our Story Since 1940

A culinary journey rooted in the historic streets of Old Delhi.

HERITAGE OF MATIA MAHAL

Serving Delhi's flavours for over 85 years.

In 1940, in the heart of Bazar Matia Mahal facing Jama Masjid Gate No. 1, Jawahar Hotel opened its kitchen. It quickly established itself as a cornerstone of Purani Dilli's food culture, frequented by generations of locals, travellers, and culinary connoisseurs.

Our recipes have been preserved with strict fidelity: gravies reduced slowly with browned onions and fresh curd, aromatic spices roasted and ground daily, and breads baked fresh in traditional tandoors.

OUR CORE PRINCIPLES

Cooking Principles

What keeps the taste consistent across every visit.

PRINCIPLE 01

Traditional Slow Simmering

Dishes like Nihari, Qorma, and Paya are slow cooked overnight in heavy brass and copper deghs to extract true flavour from bone marrow and hand-ground whole spices.

PRINCIPLE 02

Pure Ingredients

We source authentic whole spices from Asia's oldest spice markets, roasted and ground without artificial colors or preservatives.

PRINCIPLE 03

Warm Hospitality

Whether you're stopping by for a quick lunch of Qorma and Khamiri Roti or dining with extended family, our team ensures attentive, welcoming service.
