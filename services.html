What We Offer

Traditional Mughlai feasts tailored for every gathering.

Traditional Degh Catering

Full deghs of Biryani, Qorma, Nahari, and Stew prepared using wood-fire and dum techniques for weddings, celebrations, and gatherings.

  • Available in 10 kg, 20 kg, and custom bulk degh orders
  • Delivered warm and sealed for fresh dum aroma
  • Accompanied by fresh Khamiri Roti, salads, and Kheer

Family Dining

Air-conditioned dining spaces across our Jama Masjid, Okhla, and Lajpat Nagar locations for families and groups.

  • Attentive service for family gatherings
  • Fresh breads served hot straight from the tandoor
  • Clean, hygienic kitchen environment

Corporate & Group Lunches

Curated meal boxes and buffet arrangements delivered directly to office spaces and meeting venues across Delhi.

  • Customized combos (Biryani, Kebabs, and Breads)
  • Dedicated event coordination and timely delivery
  • GST billing available

Curated Takeaway

Fast-track pickup packaging that maintains heat and flavor during transit.

  • Spill-proof, food-grade thermal containers
  • Quick counter pickup at all three outlets
  • Pre-orders accepted via phone call

Specialty Roasts

Signature centerpiece preparations cooked to order for family dawats.

  • Tandoori Raan (whole leg of lamb, 20-30 min prep)
  • Murgh Musallam (stuffed whole chicken, 2 hrs prior notice)
  • Seasonal Tandoori Pomfret and Surmai

Ramadan & Seasonal Specials

Special early morning Sehri Nahari and Paya service during Ramadan, alongside Iftar dawat packages.

  • Morning Sehri Nahari & Maghaz
  • Curated Iftar packages with fresh Seekh rolls
  • Eid feast advance bookings
